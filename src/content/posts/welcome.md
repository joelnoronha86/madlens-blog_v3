# Welcome

This is a field notebook for exploring the geographies of textiles and natural dyes. Here, scientific research meets creative practice — where the chemistry of mordants and tannins sits alongside the poetry of dried plants, earth swatches, and hand-dyed fabrics.

## What to Expect

- **Scientific essays** on dye chemistry, mordanting, and colorfastness
- **Poetic observations** from the field and the dye studio
- **Visual documentation** of plants, processes, and geographies
- **Practical guides** for natural dyeing at home

## The Palette

Natural dyes offer a spectrum that synthetic colors cannot replicate. From the deep blues of indigo to the rich reds of madder, each dye tells a story of place, season, and tradition.

Explore the gallery, read the essays, and follow along as this field notebook grows.
